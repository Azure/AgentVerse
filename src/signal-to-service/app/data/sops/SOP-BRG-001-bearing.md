# SOP-BRG-001 · Rolling-element bearing degradation

## Applicability
Applies to centrifugal pumps, fans and motor-driven rotating equipment showing a
rising 1x/2x running-speed vibration signature with band energy around the bearing
defect frequencies (BPFO/BPFI). Typical symptom: vibration RMS climbing above the
warning threshold while temperature rises moderately.

## Safety
1. Lock out / tag out (LOTO) the drive and confirm zero energy.
2. Allow the bearing housing to cool below 40 °C before contact.
3. Wear cut-resistant gloves when handling races and rolling elements.

## Diagnosis confirmation
1. Capture a velocity spectrum; confirm energy at BPFO/BPFI harmonics.
2. Check lubricant for metallic particles or discolouration.
3. Verify shaft end-play and radial play against the OEM limit.

## Corrective procedure
1. Remove the coupling guard and decouple the driven shaft.
2. Extract the bearing with a proper puller — never hammer the races.
3. Clean the housing and shaft seat; inspect for fretting or bluing.
4. Install the replacement bearing using an induction heater (max 110 °C).
5. Re-lubricate to the OEM fill ratio; do not over-grease.
6. Re-couple, check alignment to 0.05 mm, and refit guards.

## Post-repair verification
1. Run at operating speed for 15 minutes; confirm vibration RMS < warning limit.
2. Log the new baseline spectrum in the CMMS work order.
