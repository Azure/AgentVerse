# SOP-IMB-004 · Rotor imbalance / mechanical looseness

## Applicability
Applies to industrial fans, impellers and rotating assemblies presenting a
dominant 1x running-speed vibration peak that scales with speed, while
temperature and current stay near nominal. Root causes include dirt build-up on
blades, lost balance weights, cracked blades or loose mounting hardware.

## Safety
1. LOTO the drive and confirm the rotor has fully stopped.
2. Secure the impeller against free rotation before working.
3. Wear eye protection when cleaning blades.

## Diagnosis confirmation
1. Confirm a dominant 1x peak in the velocity spectrum.
2. Inspect blades for uneven deposits, cracks or missing balance weights.
3. Check mounting bolts, soft foot and structural looseness.

## Corrective procedure
1. Clean deposits evenly from all blades.
2. Replace cracked or damaged blades as a matched set.
3. Torque all mounting hardware to spec; correct soft foot with shims.
4. Perform single- or two-plane field balancing to ISO 21940 grade G2.5.

## Post-repair verification
1. Run at operating speed; confirm 1x vibration is below the warning limit.
2. Record the residual imbalance and final vibration in the work order.
