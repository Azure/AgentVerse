# SOP-THM-002 · Thermal overload / overheating

## Applicability
Applies to chillers, compressors and enclosed motors where temperature rises
toward or past the alarm threshold while vibration stays comparatively low.
Root causes include fouled heat exchangers, low refrigerant charge, blocked
airflow, or degraded cooling fans.

## Safety
1. LOTO the unit and confirm zero energy before opening panels.
2. Treat all surfaces as hot; use an IR thermometer before contact.
3. For refrigerant circuits, follow F-gas handling regulations.

## Diagnosis confirmation
1. Compare suction/discharge pressures against the OEM chart.
2. Inspect condenser coils and filters for fouling or blockage.
3. Confirm cooling-fan rotation and airflow direction.
4. Check refrigerant charge and superheat.

## Corrective procedure
1. Clean condenser coils and replace clogged filters.
2. Clear any airflow obstruction around the unit.
3. Restore refrigerant charge to nameplate if low; find and fix leaks.
4. Replace failed cooling fans or contactors as required.
5. Verify thermal-overload relay settings match motor FLA.

## Post-repair verification
1. Run for 20 minutes; confirm temperature stabilises below the warning limit.
2. Record steady-state temperature and pressures in the work order.
